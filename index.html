About

freia is a Zürich-based digital creative agency at the intersection of art, technology, and research. Through visual communication, sound, music, audiovisual production, digital art, creative technology and performance, we translate complex ideas into visual, sonic and digital experiences.

Our work explores how image, sound, language and symbols interact within complex dynamic systems to shape perception, meaning and conscious experience. Through research, experimentation and emerging technologies, we co-create across cultural, scientific, educational and commercial contexts, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, inclusive communities, public engagement, sustainability and wellbeing.
