About

Independent practice / interdisciplinary work

fri28aug works across sound, language, moving image, research and digital systems — treating them as connected modes rather than separate outputs.

The practice moves between experimental culture, scientific thinking and technology. Projects explore perception, networks and complex systems, with an interest in how relationships can be made visible, audible and understandable without flattening their complexity.

Work can take the form of audiovisual pieces, editorial structures, digital prototypes, research interfaces and collaborations. Sustainability is approached in the same way: not as a separate layer, but as a network of interdependent social, ecological and economic questions.

01 / Modalities

Sound, language and moving image are treated as interacting inputs within one creative system.

02 / Systems

Research and design are used to navigate networks, dynamics, evidence and uncertainty.

03 / Sustainability

Environmental and societal relationships are explored through interconnected rather than isolated goals.
